One-minute walkthrough

01 · Read the evidence

Fit compares a fictional candidate with the role. Confidence reflects source observations; it does not identify “ghost jobs.”

02 · Compare the tradeoffs

Priority combines 50% fit, 25% confidence and 25% career value. A high total never overrides a need to verify.

03 · Choose your next move

Open the posting, verify details, then decide. Applications and messages remain human controlled.

How it works

Editable preferences → Greenhouse / Lever → Persistent observations → Eligibility and verification → Three explainable scores → Daily queue.

The private dashboard runs locally. A scheduled worker collects listings separately. This public page reads only a sanitized snapshot and stays available when upstream services fail.

No résumé uploads, private notes, login, or paid model calls are available to visitors.
